# The Moon

The moon rose over the quiet village, pale and round as a coin.
Everyone was asleep except the old watchmaker, who never slept before midnight.
He wound his clocks one last time and looked out of the window.
The night was so still that he could hear the river a mile away.

## The Silver Boat

In the garden pond something flashed, as if a star had fallen into the water.
The watchmaker put on his coat and went outside without waking anyone.
A tiny boat of silver paper was drifting between the lilies.
It carried a single passenger: a mouse in a paper hat.

The mouse took off his hat and bowed very politely.
He said that the moon had lost its reflection and needed it back before dawn.
The watchmaker asked how a reflection could be lost.
The mouse explained that a cat had shaken the pond last night.

Together they carried the boat to the middle of the pond.
The watchmaker held his lantern over the water and waited.
Slowly the moon climbed out of the river and back into the sky.
The mouse sailed home, and the clocks struck twelve.

## Morning

In the morning the villagers found the pond perfectly calm.
Nobody believed the old man when he told his story.
But from that night on he always left a saucer of milk by the gate.
And if you ever pass the village at midnight, look at the moon.
